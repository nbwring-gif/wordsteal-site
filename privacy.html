Privacy Policy for WordSteal!

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Overview

WordSteal! ("the app") is developed by Neal Wring. This app itself does not collect or store personal data, and there are no analytics or advertising services in it. Three optional features do involve data leaving your device, each described in its own section below: Apple's Game Center (leaderboards, achievements, and online matches), word definition lookups, and voice control.

Information We Collect Directly

WordSteal! itself does not collect any personal information. The app does not:

  • Collect names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Track your location
  • Access your contacts, photos, or other files
  • Use third-party analytics or advertising services

Game Center

WordSteal! uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. When you use these features:

  • Your Game Center player alias and game data (scores, achievement progress) are shared with Apple's Game Center service
  • This data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Game Center's own terms, not by WordSteal!
  • You can control your Game Center participation and privacy settings through your device's Game Center/Apple ID settings
  • If you decline to sign in to Game Center, WordSteal!'s core gameplay still works — you'll just miss out on leaderboards and achievements

Word Definitions

When you tap a word to see its meaning, that single word is sent to a free third-party dictionary service (dictionaryapi.dev) to retrieve a definition. Only the word itself is sent — no personal information, though as with any internet request the service necessarily sees your device's IP address. This feature needs an internet connection; the rest of the game works offline. Definitions are cached in memory for the current session only. The dictionary service is governed by its own terms, not by WordSteal!.

Voice Control

Voice control is optional and switched off by default. It is a per-session choice, not a saved preference: WordSteal! turns it off again every time the app launches, so it is never left running in games where you didn't ask for it.

When you do turn it on:

  • WordSteal! uses the microphone and Apple's Speech framework to interpret spoken moves and commands
  • Recognition runs on your device wherever your device supports it. Where it doesn't, the audio is processed by Apple's speech recognition service, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy rather than by WordSteal!
  • WordSteal! does not itself record, store, or transmit audio, and keeps no audio once a command has been interpreted
  • Microphone and speech recognition access are requested through the standard iOS permission prompts, and you can withdraw either at any time in Settings

Data Stored on Your Device

Local game settings and progress are stored on your device. This data never leaves your device (separate from anything synced via Game Center) and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

WordSteal! does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Game Center has its own age requirements and protections managed by Apple.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date.
