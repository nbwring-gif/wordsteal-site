WordSteal!

Build words from cards. Then take everyone else's.

WordSteal! is a word game for iPhone and iPad. You build words from the cards in your hand — but any word sitting on the table is fair game. Add a letter, rearrange the rest, and it's yours.

CARE + G  →  GRACE

Words don't stay safe. A word you built is temporary and expires before your next turn unless somebody steals it first — so the table keeps moving and nothing is ever really settled.

How you can play

  • Solo vs. AI — up to five opponents, at four levels of difficulty from the Novice Scribe to the Grand Thief.
  • Pass and play — hand the device around a table.
  • Online — turn-based matches for up to six seats through Game Center, with leaderboards and achievements. QuickFire limits make it a live game; longer limits let it run over days.
  • Hands-free voice control — say "steal cane, make crane" instead of tapping.
  • Tutorial — a guided walkthrough that teaches stealing before you meet anyone who's good at it.

Details

  • Requires iOS 16.1 or later.
  • Plays fully offline (word definitions and online matches need a connection).
  • Word validity comes from a commercially licensed NASPA word list.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Support

Something broken, confusing, or just a word you think should have been valid? Email nbwring@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version. Replies usually come within a few days.

Many questions are answered in the guides — especially the voice control and online match ones.

Why did my word disappear?
Words on the table are temporary — one expires just before its owner's next turn unless somebody steals it. Scoring happens when the word is played, so an expired word still counted.
Why was my steal rejected?
A steal has to use every letter of the target word plus at least one new letter. Reusing only part of the word, or making a word with the same letters rearranged and nothing added, isn't a steal.
The microphone pulses but nothing is transcribed.
Voice control needs Siri & Dictation enabled on the device — check Settings → Siri, Settings → General → Keyboard → Dictation, and Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → Allowed Apps. Microphone permission alone isn't enough. Voice also needs a real device; it doesn't work in the Simulator.
My online opponent hasn't moved.
Turn-based matches wait as long as the host's turn limit allows. Pull to refresh the board to re-check with Game Center. If a player leaves the match, play continues without them — the game ends only if fewer than two remain.
Where are my leaderboards and achievements?
They're reported through Game Center, so you need to be signed in. Pass-and-play games are excluded on purpose. The rest of the game works fine signed out.
Can I get my data deleted?
There's nothing to delete on our side — the app stores everything on your device, so uninstalling removes it. Game Center data is managed by Apple through your Apple ID settings.
